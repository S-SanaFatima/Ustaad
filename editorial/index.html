Trust standards

How we keep articles trustworthy

Simple rules. Easy to check. Built into every piece we publish.

01

Named authorship

Every article is written by a real person or a named group of teachers.

02

Qualified review

Every article is checked by Nida before it goes live.

03

Open profiles

Each writer has a profile you can open to see their background and articles.

04

No anonymous tips

We never publish "anonymous expert tips" with no one behind them.

  • Named authorship: Every article is written by a real person or a named group of teachers.
  • Qualified review: Every article is checked by Nida before it goes live.
  • Open profiles: Each writer has a profile you can open to see their background and articles.
  • No anonymous tips: We never publish "anonymous expert tips" with no one behind them.
