# 10 Essential IGCSE Maths Revision Tips for 2026

Preparing for your IGCSE Mathematics exam requires more than just understanding the concepts—it demands strategic revision techniques that maximize your retention and exam performance.

## 1. Start with Past Papers Early

The IGCSE Mathematics exam follows predictable patterns. By practicing past papers from 2018-2025, you'll:
- Familiarize yourself with question formats
- Identify recurring topics
- Master time management under pressure

**Pro tip**: Time yourself strictly. The IGCSE allows 2 hours for Paper 4 (Extended)—practice finishing with 15 minutes to spare.

## 2. Create a Formula Bank

While you'll have some formulas provided, knowing key ones by heart saves precious minutes:

- **Quadratic formula**: x = (-b ± √(b²-4ac)) / 2a
- **Pythagorean theorem**: a² + b² = c²
- **Trigonometric ratios**: SOH CAH TOA
- **Circle theorems**: All 8 standard theorems

Write these on flashcards and review them daily.

## 3. Focus on High-Weight Topics

Not all topics are created equal. Prioritize:

1. **Algebra** (30% of marks)
2. **Geometry & Trigonometry** (25%)
3. **Statistics** (20%)
4. **Number** (15%)
5. **Graphs & Functions** (10%)

## 4. Master the Calculator

Your calculator is a powerful tool—know how to use it efficiently:

- Store intermediate results in memory
- Use the ANS button for iterative calculations
- Practice with the exact model you'll use in the exam

## 5. Show Your Working

Examiners award method marks even if your final answer is wrong. Always:

- Write down the formula you're using
- Substitute values clearly
- State your final answer to 3 significant figures (unless told otherwise)

## 6. Practice Mental Maths

Paper 2 (Non-calculator) tests your mental arithmetic. Daily practice:

- Multiplication tables up to 15×15
- Fraction ↔ Decimal ↔ Percentage conversions
- Square numbers up to 20²

## 7. Create a Revision Timetable

Structure your final month like this:

- **Week 1-2**: Topic review + textbook exercises
- **Week 3**: Past papers (2018-2022)
- **Week 4**: Recent papers (2023-2025) + mock exam conditions

## 8. Understand Mark Schemes

Study the official mark schemes. Notice:

- Alternative methods that earn full marks
- Common mistakes that lose marks
- The level of detail required for "show that" questions

## 9. Join Study Groups

Explaining concepts to peers reinforces your own understanding. Meet weekly to:

- Solve challenging problems together
- Compare different solution methods
- Quiz each other on formulas

If you're struggling with specific topics, a specialist IGCSE Maths tutor can:

- Identify your weak areas quickly
- Provide personalized practice questions
- Teach exam-specific techniques
