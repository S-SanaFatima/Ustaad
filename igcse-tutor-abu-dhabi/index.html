How Ustaad Unlocks Full Marks on 6-Mark Questions

6-mark extended questions in Cambridge (0620/0610/0625) and Edexcel (4CH1/4BI1/4PH1) are where A* grades are decided. See how we train students to structure full-mark answers.

Sample Exam Question [6 Marks]Cambridge & Edexcel Science

"Explain, in terms of collision theory and activation energy, why increasing the temperature increases the rate of a chemical reaction."

1. Command Word & Condition Scan (1 Mark)

Identify the exact command word ("Explain", "Describe", or "Evaluate"). Highlight physical variables, units, and conditions mentioned in the prompt.

Student Answer Execution:Prompt specifies: "Explain how increasing temperature affects rate of reaction in terms of particle collision theory."
Official Mark Scheme Match:Examiner looks for: Temperature increase → Higher kinetic energy.

Exam Technique

The IGCSE Command Word Decoder

Four exam command words parents can decode in two minutes. Left shows the common misread; right shows what the mark scheme actually rewards.

"State"
Student thinks
Write a fact.
›
Mark scheme rewards
One clear sentence, no context. One mark, one answer, move on.
"Describe"
Student thinks
Write what it looks like.
›
Mark scheme rewards
Observations in order; do not include the reason yet.
"Explain"
Student thinks
Write a lot to be safe.
›
Mark scheme rewards
The reason, cause linked to effect, exact subject terminology.
"Evaluate"
Student thinks
Give an opinion.
›
Mark scheme rewards
Two sides weighed, evidence for each, conclusion in the final line.
