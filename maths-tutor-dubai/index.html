DUBAI CURRICULUM ANALYSIS

Why Maths Slips in Dubai's Mixed-Curriculum Classrooms

Dubai has more curricula sitting side by side than anywhere else in the UAE. That mix is exactly where marks get lost, not always in the topic itself.

Curriculum Transition01

Notation & Working Style Shifts

Families relocate between British, American and IB schools more often here than elsewhere in the Emirates, and notation and expected working style change with the board.

Curriculum InsightPoint 01
Multi-Exam Boards02

Single-Worksheet Half-Fit

Many Dubai schools run AQA, Edexcel, OCR and Cambridge cohorts within the same year group, so a one-size worksheet only ever half-fits.

Curriculum InsightPoint 02
Calendar Pressure03

Evening Revision Squeeze

A packed school calendar (sport, travel, activities) leaves revision squeezed into short evening windows rather than long weekend blocks.

Curriculum InsightPoint 03
Syllabus Sequence04

Mid-Year Curriculum Lag

A mid-year school change often means a new syllabus order, so a topic taught early at one school arrives late at the next.
