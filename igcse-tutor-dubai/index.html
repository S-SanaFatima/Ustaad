Common IGCSE Exam Hurdles We Fix

IGCSE exam boards test exam technique as much as subject knowledge. Our tutors address exact mark loss areas.

Six-Mark Biology Answers

Six-mark Biology questions demand exact examiner keywords rather than long descriptions.

Formula Rearranging

Stoichiometry & Moles

Chemistry mole calculations require clear multi-step workings to secure method marks.

Literature Quote Analysis

English essay responses stay thin without structured text quotes and contextual analysis.

Experimental Procedures

Timed Paper Pacing

Students run out of time on long-answer questions without disciplined paper timing practice.
